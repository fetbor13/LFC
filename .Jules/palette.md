## 2026-06-01 - [Accessible Modals & Icon Buttons]
**Learning:** Custom modals (`.modal`) in this application frequently lack `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby` attributes. Form inputs inside these modals often rely solely on placeholders without visible `<label>`s or explicit `aria-label`s. Furthermore, icon-only buttons (like the theme toggle `🌙` and modal close buttons `✕`) lack `aria-label`s. This causes severe accessibility issues for screen-reader users attempting to navigate the interface or input data.
**Action:** Always manually verify and inject `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby` onto new or existing modals. Ensure all inputs relying on placeholders receive an `aria-label` detailing their purpose, and that all icon-only buttons receive descriptive French `aria-label`s (e.g. `aria-label="Fermer"`, `aria-label="Changer de thème"`).
