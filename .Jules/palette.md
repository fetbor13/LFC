## 2024-11-20 - French ARIA Labels for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like modal close "✕" or theme toggle "🌙") across the hybrid WebOS layout often lack ARIA labels. Given the localized French context, they require specific French translations for screen reader accessibility.
**Action:** Always add standard French ARIA labels (`aria-label="Fermer"`, `aria-label="Changer de thème"`) to these common icon buttons in the HTML files.
