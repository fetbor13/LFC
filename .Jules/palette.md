## 2024-05-14 - ARIA Labels for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** In the French-language interface, icon-only buttons (like the theme toggle and modal close buttons) lacked descriptive text, making them inaccessible to screen readers.
**Action:** Always add localized `aria-label` attributes (e.g., `aria-label="Fermer"` or `aria-label="Changer de thème"`) to icon-only buttons to ensure full accessibility and compliance with UX standards.
